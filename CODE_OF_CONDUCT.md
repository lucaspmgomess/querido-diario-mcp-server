# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors, users and maintainers are expected to keep project interactions respectful, constructive and focused on the work.

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving specific and actionable technical feedback;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- respecting different levels of experience and communication styles;
- keeping issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project;
- handling security and privacy-sensitive information responsibly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without permission, or repeated bad-faith participation.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove comments, issues or contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

Moderation decisions should be based on behavior in the project, not on technical disagreement or contributor status.

## Reporting conduct issues

For sensitive conduct reports, contact **lucas.maurer@ufrgs.br** privately rather than opening a public issue.

Include enough context to understand the incident, but avoid sharing unrelated personal or sensitive information.

## Scope

This policy applies to interactions in this repository and to direct project-related communication involving maintainers or contributors.
